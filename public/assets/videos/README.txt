video-01.mp4 to video-06.mp4 (with .jpg preview pictures) are the school's built-in Campus Life videos, compressed to stay under Netlify's drag-and-drop limits (under 10 MB each).

Put your own video files here (.mp4 or .webm), for example tour.mp4.
Then in the admin dashboard open Videos and add a video with the link:
assets/videos/tour.mp4

Tips:
- Keep each file small (under about 50 MB). Long or HD videos load slowly on phones.
- For long videos, upload to YouTube (set to Unlisted if you prefer) and paste the YouTube link instead.
- After adding files here, redeploy the site so they are published.
