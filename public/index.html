    Our story

    An ultra-modern school at Mamponteng in the Kwabre East District of Kumasi, running both the Ghana Education Service (GES) and Cambridge curricula.

    Mission & vision

    To raise disciplined, curious and compassionate leaders who use knowledge and technology to serve their communities.

      Admissions

      Join the Dasco Eden family

      Apply online and upload your child's documents securely. The admissions office will call you to arrange the next steps.

      How it works

      1 · Apply onlineFill in the form below and upload your child's documents.
      2 · We contact youThe admissions office phones you on the number you give.
      3 · Visit the schoolCall +233 24 255 4403 to arrange a visit to the campus at Mamponteng.
      4 · Decision and enrolmentThe school will tell you about fees, start dates and any other requirements.

      Documents to prepare

      • Child's birth certificate
      • Passport-size photo of the child
      • Last school report (if the child has attended school)
      • Immunisation or health card (if you have it)

      Clear photos or scans in JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF format, up to 4 MB each. The school may ask for more documents. If you can't upload something now, you can bring the original to the school.

      Online application

      1. About the child

      2. Parent or guardian

      3. Documents

      Optional but recommended. Photos taken with your phone are fine; large photos are shrunk automatically.

      4. Anything else we should know?

      Questions parents ask

      Which curriculum does the school follow?

      The school runs both the Ghana Education Service (GES) and Cambridge curricula.

      Are my child's documents safe?

      Files are checked when they are uploaded and stored privately. They are never shown on the website, and only admissions staff who sign in to the school's dashboard can open them. You can ask the school to delete them at any time.

      What if the upload doesn't work?

      Your application is saved as soon as you press Submit, even if a file fails. You can retry the failed file on the next screen, or bring the original document to the school or call +233 24 255 4403.

      Where do I ask about fees and start dates?

      Fees, ages admitted and term dates are given by the admissions office. Call +233 24 255 4403 or use the quick WhatsApp enquiry below.
